This is Hollow Fox.

Hollow Fox is where I make games. It isn't a big studio, and there's no publisher behind it. It's one person who likes games that are a little odd and a lot of fun.

I like games that make you lean in. A world that's slightly off, a rule you didn't expect, a reason to try one more round. That's what I'm making here.

Why a hollow fox

Foxes are curious, quick and a little mischievous, and they come out at night. The hollow eyes are the curious part: always looking, never quite sure what's around the next corner. That's the kind of player I make these games for.

What I promise

  • Weird on purpose.Every game starts with an idea that's a little strange, then gets polished until it plays well.
  • Fair to play.No tricks to keep you hooked and no paying to win.
  • Your data stays yours.The games collect only what they need to work, and nothing is sold.

Who's behind it

Just me, making games on my own laptop at home, usually after dark, with two cats supervising. I bounce ideas off my spouse and friends, who now and then get talked into playtesting.
